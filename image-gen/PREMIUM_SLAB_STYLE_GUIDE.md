# Premium Slab Image — Design Reference

Design intent for `generate_premium_slabs.py`. Prompts are built directly in Python;
this file documents the constraints encoded in those templates.

## Material palette
Natural light grey / natural pale ash / dark charcoal / dark graphite / black.
Smooth, precision-cut slabs only. No stamped patterns, no colour washes, no warm tones.

## Shot type A — Architecture-first wide
Home fills upper 60% of frame. Slabs are the ground plane in the lower 40%.
Eye level, 24–35mm equivalent, deep depth of field.

## Shot type C — Top-angled overview
Camera 3–4m above ground, steep downward angle (within 15° of vertical).
Portrait 3:4 frame: slab geometry in lower portion, landscape context in upper portion.

## Surface aging (always applied)
One to two seasons old. Subtle tonal variation across each slab face.
Slight darkening at joints and edges. Minor efflorescence as soft lighter patches.

## Gravel realism (applied when gravel is in the layout)
Mixed stone sizes, irregular settlement, a few stones displaced at slab edges.

## Grass realism (applied when grass/lawn is in the layout)
Blade height variation, taller at untrafficked edges, a few blades overhanging slab edge.

## Always
No people. No text. No watermarks. Portrait 3:4. Photorealistic, not illustrated.
