## Gaps (none cover well) → our differentiators
1. **Deck area derived from pool length × width + deck width** (rectangle or rounded). Nobody offers it; searchers know pool size, not deck sq ft.
2. **Cost per year of service life** for every method on the same deck (lifetime value). Nobody computes it.
3. **Condition-aware recommendation** that flags overlays as unsuitable on heaving/structurally cracked slabs and prices repair.
4. **Coping replacement priced from the pool perimeter** (only Beltway mentions coping, per lf without a total).
5. **Regional multiplier** (BLS/DoD-based) instead of a single city.
6. **Shareable URL, copy result, sourced numbers** on /methodology (no competitor cites sources).
